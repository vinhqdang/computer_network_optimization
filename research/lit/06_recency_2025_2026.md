## 1. Online convex optimisation / allocation with long-term or time-varying constraints

### abdullah2026dynamic
- Citation: Mohammed Abdullah, George Iosifidis, Salah Eddine Elayoubi, Tijani Chahed (Nov 2026). "Dynamic Optimistic Constrained OCO with Memory via Delay Equivalence". NeurIPS 2026 (accepted, per arXiv comment). arXiv:2611.08302.
- Identifier / confirmation: arXiv:2611.08302; arXiv API record (first submitted 2026-11-05, last updated 2026-11-05); title, authors and date copied from the record.
- Read scope: abstract_only
- Sub-topic: 1
- Summary: Studies online convex optimisation with memory and time-varying constraints (COCO-M) and gives an optimistic algorithm using untrusted predictions, with bounds on universal dynamic regret and cumulative constraint violation. The analysis reduces memory to delayed feedback. The abstract also says the bounds adapt to non-stationarity.
- Relevance and overlap: Newest verified dynamic-regret/violation result for time-varying constraints; a citation for 'constraint violation is controlled when constraints drift'. Generic (no classes, fairness or incentives). Overlap with our method: **low**.

### aggarwal2026noisy
- Citation: Vaneet Aggarwal (Sep 2026). "Constrained Online Learning with Noisy Constraint Values". arXiv preprint (v2, Sep 2026). arXiv:2609.06921.
- Identifier / confirmation: arXiv:2609.06921; arXiv API record (first submitted 2026-09-07, last updated 2026-09-13); title, authors and date copied from the record.
- Read scope: abstract_only
- Sub-topic: 1
- Summary: Constrained OCO with adversarial constraints observed only through noisy, conditionally unbiased feedback. The LEDGER algorithm gets O(sqrt T) expected regret and O(sqrt(T log(eT))) expected budget violation (largest overspend over any window), with no Slater condition, and a Gaussian lower bound for the violation.
- Relevance and overlap: Windowed violation metric resembles our per-window SLA shortfall; noisy capacity observations match our uncertain-capacity setting. Overlap with our method: **low**.

### yu2026slater

## Does anything from 2025-2026 change the novelty picture?

No item found contains our combination: persistent tier-level virtual queues, per-device deficit queues that make real-time priority repayable, bounded class-envy, SLA and revenue constraints, and payment-free class declaration, for link or Wi-Fi capacity. Three results narrow individual claims and should be cited and contrasted. kahana2026perpetual (May 2026) controls deficits with anytime guarantees and discounted memory for fully-online fairness and names bandwidth scheduling as a motivation; we must present our contribution as the Lyapunov instantiation with tier structure, class-envy, SLA/revenue constraints and incentives, not as the idea of deficit tracking. elokda2026ev (Apr 2026) extends karma to capacity-limited charging with arrivals, departures and private deadlines, so any claim that no-money allocation with churn is unexplored must be limited to networking and class-level repayment. lin2026spec (Oct 2026) gives robust guarantees to strategic agents without money for queued demand, which strengthens the incentive related work and raises the bar for our incentive-compatibility statement.
