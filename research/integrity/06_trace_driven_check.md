## 2. Method

Code does what the text says for: window choice (six UTC days with most session starts since 2025-01-01, best minute-aligned two-hour window by concurrency), devices from sessions (t_arr=max(0,start-w0), t_dep=min(T,start+duration-w0), >=5 s), elastic cap = clip(rbar/pi_on, 0.05, 30) so mean demand = observed mean rate (pi_on=0.714), classes/tiers drawn at the synthetic probabilities, V=20 and q=0.3 unchanged, load scaling of capacity to load x Dbar, 20 elastic devices per trace with stay >= 60 s. Volume of a deviating device is capped by its true demand (usable = min(rate, cap) when declared RT; sim.py line 186), so no artefact there; truthful reruns reproduce part 1 exactly (crosscheck 0.0). B(g,video)=V w/rmin+rmax = 43/63/83 is correct.

Undisclosed choices / issues that the text should state:

a. Window truncation. 118 of 283 devices (k=0; 81-126 over traces) are already present at slot 0 and 117 still present at T; 23-42 per trace span the whole window. They enter the simulator as fresh arrivals with zero deficits and all arrive in the same slot. The "stays" (median 2078-2880 s, 35-48 min) and the "stay of two hours" are in-window segments, not session lengths. Four of the six windows are consecutive Thursdays (2026-04-09, 16, 23, 30), so the six traces are not independent days and the t-intervals over six traces understate between-day variation. The windows are the busiest days by construction (not typical).
b. Session semantics. Dataset note: connections are limited to 2 h, reconnection is free; 20% of PW0127 sessions exceed 7200 s (segments). A "device" is one session, so a reconnecting user is a new device. Times are minute-truncated for ~91% of rows (start truncated, duration in seconds); end = start + duration, so edge times are +-60 s uncertain. Timestamps are all labelled +00:00; if they are local time it is immaterial (activity is 05-22 h, no day-boundary effect) but should be stated as "taken as UTC".
c. HSDPA mapping. The text says "per-second throughput logs". Code treats each log line as one slot irrespective of its duration column and timestamp. Over the 78 files used: median line 1.01 s, but 2.6% of lines last >1.5 s (up to 995 s) and the logs contain 87,832 s of span for 75,304 lines, i.e. ~14% of wall time has no line and is silently collapsed (540 negative timestamp steps as well). Hence true outages (no data) are removed; only 0.007% of lines have zero bytes, and 2-5% of the scaled slots are below 5% of the mean. "outages in tunnels" is not what the series shows; say "deep fades". The tram.jernbanetorget-universitetssykehuset logs in data/raw/hsdpa are not used (ROUTES lists 7 of 8 routes); undisclosed. The same capacity shapes (seeds 7000+k) are used for both sites (identical CVs), so the second site is not independent as a check on capacity.
d. What the logs determine. Observed data fix only the elastic share of nominal demand: 34-48% of Dbar per trace; the rest is random video (1.5 Mbit/s) / gaming. Capacity is scaled to Dbar, so "load 1" is set mostly by invented RT demand, not by observed bytes. 6-10% of elastic caps are clipped at 0.05 or 30.
e. Site descriptors "youth centre" (PW0127) and "museum" (PW0232) appear nowhere in the repository or data; I could not confirm them (site list endpoints unreachable). "the site with the largest population" is true only among the two downloaded sites (358k vs 226k sessions); the code never ranked all sites.
f. The three devices above the bound (k=0 dev 52, tier free, L=7200, ell 1.19, gain 91.8, 2.14 B; k=2 dev 210, L=4740, gain 66.7, 1.55 B; k=1 dev 218, L=2760, gain 64.0, 1.49 B). Not a measurement artefact or noise: volume is demand-capped, the truthful rerun reproduces the stored value, and a 0.05% perturbation of capacity in one slot changes the three devices' volumes by <=0.2 Mbit (versus 64-92 Mbit gains). But the text's explanation "gain per slot ... accumulates over thousands of slots" is not supported as stated. For device 52, declaring video for only the first, second or last third of the stay changes volume by -3.4, -37.0 and +8.4 Mbit (truthful 3820.2), whereas declaring for the whole stay gives +91.8. The gain is not additive in slots; it appears only with sustained declaration (an interaction with the deficit/price dynamics, plausibly the price-taking failure, but not shown). Also these devices have high-ish ell and long stays and are the free tier (B=43, the smallest bound). Suggested text: "...the gain is not proportional to the length of the deviation (declaring video for a third of the stay gains nothing), so we cannot attribute it to simple accumulation; it requires sustained declaration".

## 3. Wording

- "We now replace all four by measurements" and "traces built from measurements": overclaims. Arrivals/stays/demand levels come from Paris Wi-Fi logs, capacity from 2010-11 Oslo 3G commute logs that have no relation to them, classes/tiers/on-off processes and RT demands are random. Use "trace-driven" and "built from public logs", and say capacity is borrowed from an unrelated network.
- "The ordering of the rules is the same as on synthetic traces": unqualified; the comparison is for the rules' rank on RT slots in this table only (Credit and capped priority differ), and the second site only shows REPAY > PF and cap < REPAY.
- "The capped priority ... does not transfer" and "A cap chosen for one traffic mix is thus not right for another": generalises from one cap value on one site-type (second site: cap serves 82.0% vs REPAY 85.1%, which is consistent but premium ratio there was not reported). Restrict to "q=0.3 does not transfer to these traces".
- "truthful declaration is not a best response for most devices, only nearly one": tested only against a single deviation (declare video for the whole stay), 20 devices per trace sampled with stay >= 60 s.
- Abstract "traces built from public Wi-Fi session logs and measured mobile capacity, where the ordering of the other rules is unchanged": acceptable but "measured mobile capacity" should be "mobile capacity logs"; "ordering unchanged" as above.
- Abstract "On the measured traces, where stays last three quarters of an hour": "measured" -> "trace-driven"; stays are in-window medians of 35-48 min (mean over traces 42 min).
- Discussion "stays of up to two hours": window-truncated, fine if stated.
- Discussion "three devices ... by a few per cent": see 1.7.
- Caption of real_traces and text should state that the six days are the busiest, and four are consecutive weeks.
